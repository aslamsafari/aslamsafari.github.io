About

Dr. Muhammad Aslam Mohd Safari is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics, Faculty of Science, Universiti Putra Malaysia. He completed his PhD and MSc in Statistics and his BSc in Actuarial Science at Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia.

His research develops and applies statistical models for complex real-world data, particularly where extremes, outliers, heavy tails, uncertainty, and environmental risk are central to the analysis.

Research

Extreme Value Modelling

Statistical modelling of extreme wind speeds, temperatures, air pollution episodes, and tail behaviour in environmental and climate data.

Robust Statistical Estimation

Robust estimation methods for Weibull, Pareto-type, Lindley, and related distributions under outliers and heavy-tailed data.

Applied Statistical Modelling

Applications in income distribution and inequality, reliability analysis, air quality risk, wind energy assessment, and interdisciplinary data analysis.
